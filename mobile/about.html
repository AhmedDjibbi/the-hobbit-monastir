Our Heritage & Vision

Forged in Warmth, Brewed with Devotion

Nestled in the heart of Monastir, The Hobbit was born from a simple yearning: to create a sanctuary away from the clamor of the modern world. Here, every roast tells a story of craftsmanship, and every corner offers a seat by the hearth for weary travelers and adventurous spirits alike.
